# Participant Onboarding Subcommittee

## Purpose

This subcommittee ensures that all project leads are onboarded and have required information needed to engage and onboard their team members, including remote attendees, who are fully oriented and prepared before the event begins. It develops materials, conducts orientation sessions, and follows up with teams to confirm readiness.

## Responsibilities

* Develop and deliver participant and project lead onboarding materials
* Conduct orientation sessions outlining expectations, timelines, and workflows
* Coordinate onboarding for remote and in-person participants
* Support project leads in understanding BioHackathon execution requirements
* Confirm that all participants are onboarded before the event start

## Success Metrics

* All project leads complete onboarding before team members get onboarded
* Support project leads to orient their team members before the event start
* Reduced volume of pre-event onboarding-related questions
* Positive participant feedback on onboarding clarity and effectiveness
