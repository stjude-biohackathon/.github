# Project Judging Subcommittee

## Purpose

This subcommittee designs and executes the judging process for all BioHackathon project teams. It establishes criteria, assigns judges, coordinates the evaluation schedule on Day 3 (September 18), validates results, and facilitates the awards ceremony

## Responsibilities

* Establish judging criteria and scoring frameworks
* Confirm teams and assign judges
* Coordinate judging logistics and evaluation schedules
* Validate final scores and resolve any scoring disputes
* Announce winners at the closing ceremony on September 18, 2026

## Success Metrics

* Judging completed on schedule without scoring disputes
* Clear and documented scoring outcomes
* Positive judge and participant feedback on evaluation fairness
