# Projects Review Subcommittee

## Purpose
This subcommittee manages the full challenge selection and team assignment pipeline — from reviewing submitted challenges against established criteria, to forming balanced, multidisciplinary teams from the registered participant pool.

## Responsibilities

* Review and evaluate challenge submissions against established scoping rubrics
* Review and evaluate challenge submissions
* Approve, request revisions, combine similar ideas or decline submissions with feedback
* Engage with the onboarding team and support challenge proposer in identifying co-leads
* Assign participants to balanced, multidisciplinary teams
* Support onboarding of challenge owners and team leads

## Success Metrics

* High-quality, feasible challenges identified and team-leads allocated
* Teams finalized by August 14, 2026
* Balanced team composition across skills and backgrounds
