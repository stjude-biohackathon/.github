# St. Jude BioHackathon Scientific Committee

## Purpose
The Scientific Committee ensures the scientific integrity, fairness, and technical readiness of the BioHackathon. 
It oversees the entire challenge-to-team-to-judging pipeline: from reviewing challenge submissions and assigning teams, to ensuring participants are onboarded and technically equipped, to coordinating fair evaluation of all projects. Through a combination of structured project review, participant onboarding, and thoughtful preparation of data and tools, the committee helps create an environment where innovative ideas can be explored effectively.

## Key Responsibilities

* Oversee the quality and integrity of submitted BioHackathon challenges
* Coordinate team formation and participant assignment (finalized August 14, 2026)
* Support onboarding of team leads and challenge owners
* Review and approve/deny travel awardee applications
* Ensure all technical requirements are met before the event
* Oversee judging coordination and evaluation processes

## How does the committee operate?

The Scientific Committee operates through four subcommittees:
* [Projects Review Subcommittee](bh-subcommittees/projects-review-subcommittee.md)
* [Participant Onboarding Subcommittee](bh-subcommittees/participant-onboarding-subcommittee.md)
* [Technical Support Subcommittee](bh-subcommittees/technical-support-subcommittee.md)
* [Project Judging Subcommittee](bh-subcommittees/project-judging-subcommittee.md)

The committees has been joined by the Scientific Committee Members, each member participating actively in 1-2 subcommittees.

Each subcommittee has a designated Chairs and coordinate with the overall Scientific Committee Chair (Malvika Sharan, PhD).
Ariel Maclin serves as operational liaison across all four.
